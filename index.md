This project is a miniature replica of the classic Macintosh, running Basilisk II on a Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W, and using a Waveshare 2.8" LCD display as the screen.

# Sinister but cute. Cute but sinister.
![](/media/basilisk-hatchling.png)

The inspiration for this project came from this video by [@ThisDoesNotCompute](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dRr5iVjMfqs).
He has done an amazing job finding the right parts and putting everything together, however, I felt that his choice of the OS and emulator was not optimal, and I decided to do it my own way. (Narrator: little did he know...)
